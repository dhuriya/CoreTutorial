localStorage	                    Backend nahi banayenge, isliye browser me data save karenge. Real project me ye JWT token hota hai
Reactive Forms	                    Validation easy (Validators.required, Validators.email), aur dynamic form control
AuthGuard	                        /dashboard route ko protect karega — bina login ke koi andar na ja sake
Standalone Components	            Angular 17+ ka modern way — NgModule ki zaroorat nahi
