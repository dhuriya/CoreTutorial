# 90 Days English Fluency Challenge — Practice Script

*(Punctuated and paragraphed for speaking practice. Read each line aloud, pausing at commas and stopping fully at full stops.)*

## Introduction

Welcome to the 90 Days English Fluency Challenge. Are you ready to take your English skills from basic to fluent in just 3 months? This series is designed with one goal in mind: making you fluent, confident, and ready to speak English in any situation.

With short, practical lessons released three times a week, you'll dive into real life topics — work, travel, conversations with friends, shopping, and more. Practice each video at least nine times, review on Sundays, and watch your English transform.

First, let's learn how to start the journey.

## The Challenge — Explained

**John:** Hello, Sarah. Are you ready for the 90 Days English Fluency Challenge?

**Sarah:** Hi! Yes, I want to become fluent in English. How does the challenge work?

**John:** Great, here's how it works. If you practice each video at least nine times or more, you'll become fluent in just 90 days.

**Sarah:** Really? Just by practicing the videos?

**John:** Yes. We'll upload new videos on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday each week, so you'll have two days to practice each one. Plus, Sundays are for revision.

**Sarah:** That sounds manageable. How many videos will there be?

**John:** There will be a total of 90 videos. They'll cover essential topics like work, travel, general English, friendly chitchat, shopping, and many more areas useful in daily life.

**Sarah:** That's perfect. Will there be different levels?

**John:** Absolutely. We'll have conversations for beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels. These will include more than 5,000 words commonly used in daily communication.

**Sarah:** Wow, that's a lot of vocabulary!

**John:** Yes, and you'll also learn over 50,000 sentences, both short and long, along with phrasal verbs, idioms, and phrases.

**Sarah:** This sounds like the perfect course.

**John:** Yes, if you understand a little bit of English.

**Sarah:** Yes, I understand some English, but I want to be fluent.

**John:** Then this is perfect for you. Stick with it, practice daily, and you'll see amazing progress. Ready to get started?

**Sarah:** Yes, I'm excited to begin, but I don't know how to practice.

**John:** Let me tell you a very effective way to practice.

**Sarah:** Yes, I want to know the best way.

**John:** The best and very effective way is to listen and repeat.

**Sarah:** What do you mean, exactly?

**John:** Listen to others and repeat what they say. It's called the shadowing technique.

**Sarah:** Does that really work?

**John:** Yes, it's a very effective way to learn any language.

**Sarah:** Is that all?

**John:** No, that's the first, but very important, step.

**Sarah:** Oh, and what are the other steps?

**John:** I'll tell you later. Let's complete the first step.

**Sarah:** Okay.

**John:** Okay, in this lesson we'll listen to two speakers talking with clear pronunciation. You can choose any speaker you want and repeat what the speaker says.

**Sarah:** Okay.

**John:** But keep that in mind — speak clear and loud, and try to match the rhythm, pronunciation, and intonation with the speaker.

**Sarah:** Okay, yes, got it.

**John:** In this video, you will get to learn very essential questions and answers about daily life to speak fluent English.

**Sarah:** Really?

**John:** Yes. And please keep that in mind — it takes a lot of effort and time to create such a video. Your likes and comments motivate us to create more videos, so please like the video if you haven't yet, drop a comment below, and share it with your fellow learners.

Let's get started.

## Conversation 1 — Meeting Someone New (Beginner Level)

**John:** Hello, I'm John.

**Sarah:** Oh, hi, I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.

**John:** Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?

**Sarah:** I'm from San Francisco. And you?

**John:** I'm from India. What do you do for a living?

**Sarah:** I'm a student. Do you have any hobbies?

**John:** Yes, I like reading and painting. Do you like to exercise?

**Sarah:** Yes, I enjoy staying active. Are you a morning person?

**John:** No, I prefer evenings. Do you like to dance?

**Sarah:** Yes, I love dancing. How about attending concerts?

**John:** No, I'm not a fan of concerts. Are you free this weekend?

**Sarah:** Yes, I am. What's the plan?

**John:** Would you like to go out for coffee?

**Sarah:** Yes, I'd love to. When?

**John:** How about this Saturday?

**Sarah:** Yes, that works for me. Do you like surprises?

**John:** Yes, I enjoy surprises. Do you like going to the movies?

**Sarah:** Yes, I love it. What kind of movies do you like?

**John:** I enjoy action and comedy movies. Do you have any pets?

**Sarah:** No, I don't have any pets. What's your favorite season?

**John:** I love spring, because the weather is nice. Do you like online shopping?

**Sarah:** Yes, it's convenient for me. How about cooking new recipes?

**John:** No, I prefer simple meals. What are your plans for the weekend?

**Sarah:** I plan to relax or go out with friends. Are you doing anything fun today?

**John:** Not really, just staying in. Do you like camping?

**Sarah:** Yes, I love camping trips. How about fishing?

**John:** No, I'm not into fishing. Do you play any instruments?

**Sarah:** Yes, I play guitar. Do you like attending parties?

**John:** Yes, I enjoy social events. How about public speaking?

**Sarah:** No, I'm not comfortable with it. Are you on social media?

**John:** Yes, I use Instagram. Do you like technology?

**Sarah:** No, I'm not very tech-savvy. How about board games?

**John:** No, I prefer video games. Do you like studying?

**Sarah:** Yes, I enjoy learning new things. Do you prefer tea or coffee?

**John:** I prefer coffee. Do you like spicy food?

**Sarah:** Yes, I enjoy a bit of spice. How about sweet desserts?

**John:** No, I'm not a fan of sweets. Do you like rainy days?

**Sarah:** Yes, I find them cozy. How about watching sports?

**John:** No, I'm not a sports fan. Do you like studying languages?

**Sarah:** Yes, I find it challenging. Do you like reading books or watching movies?

**John:** I enjoy both, but I prefer movies. How about public transportation?

**Sarah:** No, I prefer driving. Do you like cold weather?

**John:** No, I prefer warmer days. How about indoor activities?

**Sarah:** No, I prefer outdoor activities. Do you want to study further?

**John:** Yes, I'm considering it. How often do you travel?

**Sarah:** I try to travel once a year. Are you planning to travel soon?

**John:** Yes, I'm planning to visit Paris. Do you like art museums?

**Sarah:** Yes, I appreciate art. What's your favorite type of food?

**John:** I love Italian food. Do you like spicy food?

**Sarah:** No, I can't handle spice. Are you a vegetarian?

**John:** No, I'm not. Do you like solving puzzles?

**Sarah:** Yes, I find it relaxing. How about roller coasters?

**John:** No, I don't like them. Do you like winter sports?

**Sarah:** Yes, I enjoy skiing. Are you learning anything new?

**John:** Yes, I'm studying a new language. Do you know how to cook?

**Sarah:** Yes, I cook often. Have you tried learning online?

**John:** Yes, it's very convenient.

**Lisa:** Yes, I love dancing. Do you enjoy going to concerts?

**Mark:** Not really, I prefer smaller events. What about surprises, do you like them?

**Lisa:** Yes, I enjoy a good surprise. Would you call yourself a morning person?

**Mark:** Nope, I'm more of a night owl. Do you like online shopping?

**Lisa:** Yes, it's super convenient. How about trying new recipes?

**Mark:** Not really, I stick to basics. Do you enjoy camping?

**Lisa:** Yes, I love it. And fishing?

**Mark:** Not a fan of fishing. How about attending parties?

**Lisa:** Yes, I enjoy going out with friends. Are you comfortable with public speaking?

**Mark:** Not really, I get pretty nervous. Do you like technology?

**Lisa:** Yes, I think it's fascinating. How about board games?

**Mark:** No, I prefer outdoor activities. Do you study much?

**Lisa:** Yes, I love learning new things. Are you a singer?

**Mark:** Yes, I love singing. Do you watch sports?

**Lisa:** Not much, actually. How about spicy food?

**Mark:** Yes, I enjoy spicy dishes. Sweet desserts?

**Lisa:** No, I don't have much of a sweet tooth. Rainy days?

**Mark:** Yes, I find them cozy. Public transportation?

**Lisa:** Not really, I prefer to drive. Do you enjoy studying languages?

**Mark:** Yes, I find it challenging and rewarding. How about indoor activities?

**Lisa:** I prefer being outdoors. Do you visit art museums?

**Mark:** Yes, I love exploring art. And solving puzzles?

**Lisa:** Yes, it's very relaxing for me. Do you enjoy roller coasters?

**Mark:** No, they're too scary. How about winter sports?
