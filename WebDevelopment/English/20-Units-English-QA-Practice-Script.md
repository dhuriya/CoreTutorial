*(Punctuated and paragraphed for speaking practice. Read each line aloud, pausing at commas and stopping fully at full stops.)*

## Unit 1 — Where Do You Come From?

**A:** Hello.

**B:** Hello. How are you?

**A:** I'm fine, thank you. And you?

**B:** I'm fine, thank you. Where are you from?

**A:** I come from England.

**B:** You come from England. Do you like Manchester United?

**A:** Um, I don't like football.

**B:** Oh, you don't like football? I see. Why did you come here?

**A:** I came for vacation.

**B:** You came for vacation? Are you having fun?

**A:** Yes, I'm having a great time.

**B:** What do you like the best about here?

**A:** What do I like the best? Well, the people are very kind.

**B:** Yes, the people here are kind. Do you like the food?

**A:** Yes, the food is great.

**B:** Is the food too spicy for you?

**A:** No, I love spicy food. I can eat spicy food every day.

**B:** How much longer will you stay here?

**A:** I will stay two more days.

**B:** Oh, then you will go home?

**A:** Yes, then I will go home.

**B:** Where do you work?

**A:** I work for a big company.

**B:** What kind of company do you work for?

**A:** I work for an import company. And what about you?

**B:** I work at a bank.

**A:** Huh? Are you a bank teller?

**B:** No, I'm the owner.
