## Deleting Instagram — The Story Behind the Break

**Jay:** And was there ever a piece of feedback, or a rumor, or something you saw that did affect you, where you actually thought, "well, at least — I'm trying to see the positives, but this one's tough," something that made you have to pause and figure it out?
